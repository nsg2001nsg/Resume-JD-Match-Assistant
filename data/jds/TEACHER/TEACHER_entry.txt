Job Title: Junior Teacher / Assistant Faculty
Company: Bright Horizons School
Location: Pune, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring a Junior Teacher to deliver engaging classroom instruction, support student learning, and contribute to our school's academic programs across primary or secondary grades.

Responsibilities:
- Plan and deliver lesson plans aligned with CBSE or ICSE curriculum
- Create a positive and inclusive learning environment in the classroom
- Assess student performance through assignments, tests, and observations

- Maintain class records, attendance, and progress reports
- Participate in school events, parent meetings, and professional development programs

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Education (B.Ed.) or subject specialization with B.Ed.
- 0-2 years of teaching experience at primary or secondary level
- Strong subject knowledge in Mathematics, Science, English, or Social Studies
- Ability to design engaging lesson plans and classroom activities
- Excellent communication and classroom management skills
- Patience, empathy, and genuine passion for student development

Preferred:
- Experience with digital teaching tools (Google Classroom, smart boards)
- Knowledge of activity-based learning or Montessori methods
