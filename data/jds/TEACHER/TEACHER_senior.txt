Job Title: Principal / Head of Academics
Company: Meridian International School
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 15+ years

Role Overview:
We are looking for an experienced academic leader to serve as Principal, leading our school's educational vision, managing faculty, and ensuring the highest standards of student learning and institutional excellence.

Responsibilities:
- Define and execute the school's academic vision, curriculum strategy, and quality standards
- Lead, mentor, and evaluate all teaching and non-teaching staff
- Drive student outcomes improvement through data-led academic interventions
- Manage school administration including admissions, timetabling, and compliance
- Build relationships with parents, education boards, and community stakeholders
- Drive accreditation, quality audits, and school improvement plans
- Represent the institution in educational forums and parent bodies

Requirements:
- Master's degree in Education or relevant academic discipline
- B.Ed. mandatory; M.Ed. or Ph.D. preferred
- 15+ years of teaching with at least 5 years in academic leadership
- Deep expertise in CBSE, ICSE, IB, or Cambridge curriculum frameworks
- Strong leadership, strategic thinking, and stakeholder communication
- Proven track record improving student outcomes and institutional rankings

Preferred:
- Experience managing an internationally accredited school
- Published educational research or curriculum development contributions
