Job Title: Project Manager / Construction Manager
Company: Techbuild Constructions Ltd.
Location: Pune, India
Experience Required: 6-10 years

Role Overview:
We are looking for an experienced Project Manager to lead construction projects from planning to handover, managing timelines, budgets, and quality across residential and commercial developments.

Responsibilities:
- Lead end-to-end project execution from mobilization to commissioning
- Manage project schedules using MS Project or Primavera P6
- Coordinate with design consultants, MEP teams, and subcontractors
- Monitor and control project costs, variations, and change orders
- Ensure compliance with building codes, safety standards, and quality norms
- Conduct client meetings and provide regular project status updates
- Manage manpower planning and contractor relationships

Requirements:
- B.E. or M.Tech in Civil Engineering
- 6-10 years of construction project management experience
- Strong knowledge of project management methodologies and tools
- Proficiency in AutoCAD, Revit, Primavera, or MS Project
- Understanding of contracts, BOQ preparation, and cost management
- Excellent leadership and stakeholder communication skills

Preferred:
- PMP certification preferred
- Experience with LEED or green building projects
