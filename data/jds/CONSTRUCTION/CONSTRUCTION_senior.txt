Job Title: Director of Projects / VP Construction
Company: Landmark Developers Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 15+ years

Role Overview:
We are seeking a Director of Projects to oversee our entire construction portfolio, drive delivery excellence, and provide strategic leadership across our residential, commercial, and infrastructure verticals.

Responsibilities:
- Lead and manage multiple concurrent construction projects across India
- Define project delivery standards, policies, and best practices
- Drive cost optimization, risk management, and quality assurance frameworks
- Build and mentor a high-performing project management team
- Manage relationships with architects, consultants, government bodies, and clients
- Oversee all contract negotiations, vendor management, and procurement
- Report project portfolio performance to board and senior leadership

Requirements:
- B.E. or M.Tech in Civil Engineering; MBA preferred
- 15+ years of progressive experience in construction project management
- Proven track record delivering large-scale projects on time and budget
- Deep expertise in contracts, risk management, and cost control
- Strong leadership, strategic planning, and executive communication skills
- Knowledge of RERA, building regulations, and environmental compliance

Preferred:
- PMP or PRINCE2 certification
- International construction project experience
