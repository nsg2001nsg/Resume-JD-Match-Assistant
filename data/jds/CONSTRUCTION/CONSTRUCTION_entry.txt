Job Title: Site Engineer / Graduate Engineer Trainee
Company: BuildRight Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Hyderabad, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring a Site Engineer to support construction operations on active project sites, assist in technical coordination, and ensure work is executed as per design drawings and specifications.

Responsibilities:
- Supervise daily construction activities on site
- Ensure work is executed as per approved drawings and specifications
- Coordinate with subcontractors and labor for task execution
- Maintain daily site records, progress reports, and material logs
- Assist in quality checks and inspection activities
- Support senior engineers in resolving technical issues on site

Requirements:
- B.E. or B.Tech in Civil Engineering
- 0-2 years of experience in construction or infrastructure projects
- Basic knowledge of construction materials, methods, and building codes
- Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings
- Familiarity with AutoCAD and MS Project
- Willingness to relocate to project sites

Preferred:
- Exposure to residential, commercial, or infrastructure projects
- Knowledge of RERA compliance and IS codes
