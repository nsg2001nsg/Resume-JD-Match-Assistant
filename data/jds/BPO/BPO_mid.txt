Job Title: BPO Team Lead / Operations Supervisor
Company: GlobalConnect BPO Solutions
Location: Pune, India
Experience Required: 3-6 years

Role Overview:
We are looking for an experienced Team Lead to manage a team of customer service agents, drive process KPIs, and ensure client SLA compliance across our international BPO operations.

Responsibilities:
- Lead and manage a team of 15-20 customer support agents
- Monitor real-time queue performance and ensure SLA adherence
- Conduct quality audits, feedback sessions, and coaching for agents
- Prepare and present daily/weekly performance reports to operations management
- Handle escalated customer complaints and ensure resolution
- Drive process improvements and reduce average handle time
- Coordinate with training team for new hire onboarding

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in any discipline
- 3-6 years of BPO experience with at least 1-2 years in a team lead role
- Strong knowledge of contact center metrics (AHT, CSAT, FCR, shrinkage)
- Proficiency in workforce management and CRM tools
- Excellent coaching, communication, and conflict resolution skills
- Experience in international voice or blended processes

Preferred:
- Six Sigma or Lean certification is a plus
- Experience with Avaya, Cisco, or Genesys call platforms
