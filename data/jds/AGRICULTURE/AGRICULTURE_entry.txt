Job Title: Agriculture Field Officer
Company: GreenRoot Agri Solutions
Location: Nashik, Maharashtra
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring an Agriculture Field Officer to support farmers in adopting modern farming techniques, crop management practices, and sustainable agriculture methods across our operational districts.

Responsibilities:
- Conduct field visits to farms and advise on crop cultivation practices
- Assist farmers with soil testing, seed selection, and fertilizer recommendations
- Collect crop data and maintain field records
- Support implementation of government agricultural schemes
- Educate farmers on integrated pest management and irrigation practices
- Report field observations to the regional agriculture manager

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Agriculture (B.Sc. Agriculture) from a recognized university
- 0-2 years of field experience in agronomy or crop science
- Knowledge of major Kharif and Rabi crops
- Familiarity with soil health management and fertilizer applications
- Basic computer skills and ability to maintain digital records
- Willingness to travel extensively to rural areas
- Proficiency in Marathi or Hindi preferred

Preferred:
- Exposure to precision agriculture or drip irrigation systems
- Knowledge of government agricultural subsidy programs
