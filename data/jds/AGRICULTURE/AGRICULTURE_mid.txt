Job Title: Agronomist / Farm Manager
Company: Kisan Tech Farms Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Hyderabad, Telangana
Experience Required: 4-7 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking an experienced Agronomist to manage our farm operations, optimize crop yields, and implement advanced agricultural practices across our 500-acre facility and partner farms.

Responsibilities:
- Plan and manage full crop production cycles from sowing to harvest
- Design and implement integrated crop management programs
- Monitor soil health, water management, and nutrient programs
- Evaluate and introduce new crop varieties and farming technologies
- Coordinate with vendors for procurement of seeds, fertilizers, and equipment
- Train and supervise farm laborers and junior field staff
- Maintain production records and prepare crop performance reports

Requirements:
- B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Agriculture, Agronomy, or Horticulture
- 4-7 years of experience in farm management or agronomy
- Strong knowledge of crop physiology, soil science, and pest management
- Experience with drip irrigation, fertigation, and greenhouse operations
- Ability to analyze soil and crop data to drive decisions
- Strong organizational and leadership skills

Preferred:
- Exposure to organic farming or GAP certification processes
- Experience with farm management software or precision agriculture tools
