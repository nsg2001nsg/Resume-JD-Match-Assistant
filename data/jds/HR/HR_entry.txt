Job Title: HR Executive / Talent Acquisition Associate
Company: PeopleFirst HR Services
Location: Gurgaon, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring an HR Executive to support talent acquisition, employee onboarding, and core HR operations across our expanding organization.

Responsibilities:
- Source and screen candidates for open roles across functions
- Coordinate interview scheduling and candidate communication
- Support onboarding, documentation, and induction programs for new hires
- Maintain employee records in HRMS and ensure data accuracy
- Assist in payroll inputs, attendance management, and leave tracking
- Support employee engagement initiatives and HR events
- Handle employee queries related to policies and benefits

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or Psychology
- 0-2 years of HR experience or relevant internship
- Basic knowledge of recruitment, onboarding, and HR processes
- Familiarity with HRMS tools (Zoho People, Darwinbox, or SAP SuccessFactors)
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills
- High attention to detail and confidentiality

Preferred:
- MBA or PGDM in HR from a recognized institution
- Experience with job portals like Naukri, LinkedIn, and Shine
