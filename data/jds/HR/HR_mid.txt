Job Title: HR Business Partner / Senior HR Manager
Company: GlobalTech India Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 5-8 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking an experienced HR Business Partner to align people strategy with business objectives, manage the full employee lifecycle, and drive engagement and retention programs across assigned business units.

Responsibilities:
- Partner with business leaders on workforce planning, talent acquisition, and org design
- Manage end-to-end recruitment for senior and specialist roles
- Lead performance management cycles, compensation reviews, and career development programs
- Drive employee engagement surveys and action planning
- Handle complex employee relations cases and disciplinary processes
- Ensure compliance with labor laws, statutory requirements, and company policies
- Develop and implement HR policies, processes, and initiatives

Requirements:
- MBA or MSW in Human Resources
- 5-8 years of generalist HR or HRBP experience
- Strong knowledge of Indian labor laws (Shops & Establishments, PF, ESI, Gratuity)
- Proficiency in HRMS platforms (SAP SuccessFactors, Workday, or Darwinbox)
- Excellent stakeholder management and business partnering skills
- Data-driven approach to HR metrics and workforce analytics

Preferred:
- SHRM-CP or PHRi certification
- Experience in technology, manufacturing, or BFSI sectors
