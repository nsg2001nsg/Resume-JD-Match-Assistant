Job Title: CHRO / Head of Human Resources
Company: Nexus Corporate Group
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 15+ years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a CHRO to define and execute our people strategy, build an exceptional organizational culture, and drive talent and leadership development across our 5,000-person enterprise.

Responsibilities:
- Define the organization's people strategy aligned with business goals
- Lead all HR functions including talent acquisition, L&D, compensation, HRBP, and HR ops
- Drive culture transformation, diversity and inclusion, and engagement programs
- Build leadership development and succession planning frameworks
- Manage total rewards, compensation benchmarking, and benefits strategy
- Oversee HR compliance, labor relations, and statutory obligations
- Partner with the CEO and Board on organizational effectiveness

Requirements:
- MBA or Master's in HR, Organizational Psychology, or related field
- 15+ years of progressive HR experience with at least 5 years as HR head
- Proven track record leading HR transformation in large organizations
- Deep expertise in talent strategy, culture building, and change management
- Strong executive presence, board-level communication, and business acumen
- Experience managing HR across multi-state or international operations

Preferred:
- SHRM-SCP or GPHR certification
- Experience leading HR through M&A, IPO, or rapid scaling phases
