Job Title: Senior Automobile Engineer / Service Manager
Company: DriveMax Automotive Solutions
Location: Pune, India
Experience Required: 5-8 years

We are looking for an experienced Automobile Engineer to manage our service division, lead technical operations, and ensure high customer satisfaction across our multi-brand service centers.

- Oversee day-to-day operations of the vehicle service center
- Diagnose complex mechanical, electrical, and electronic faults

- B.E. or Diploma in Automobile or Mechanical Engineering
- 5-8 years of experience in automobile servicing or workshop management
- Strong expertise in vehicle diagnostics across ICE and electric vehicles
- Proficiency with diagnostic equipment (ADAS calibration, oscilloscopes)
- Leadership and team management experience
- Customer-facing communication and problem-solving skills

Preferred:
- OEM-certified training (Mercedes, BMW, or Tata EV programs)
- Experience with dealership management systems (DMS)
