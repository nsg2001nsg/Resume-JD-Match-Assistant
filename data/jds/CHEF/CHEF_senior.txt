Job Title: Executive Chef / Head of Culinary Operations
Company: Prestige Hospitality Group
Location: Delhi, India
Experience Required: 12+ years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a seasoned Executive Chef to lead culinary operations across our portfolio of restaurants and banquet facilities, drive menu innovation, and maintain the highest food quality standards.

Responsibilities:
- Lead culinary teams across multiple outlets and banquet operations
- Develop seasonal menus, food concepts, and pricing strategies
- Drive food cost optimization and kitchen profitability
- Establish and enforce food safety, hygiene, and HACCP standards
- Collaborate with F&B Director on revenue strategy and guest experience
- Build and mentor the culinary talent pipeline
- Represent the brand at media events and food festivals

Requirements:
- Diploma or degree in Culinary Arts or Hotel Management
- 12+ years of culinary experience with at least 5 years as Executive Chef
- Proven expertise in multi-outlet kitchen management
- Strong menu development, food costing, and P&L management skills
- Excellent leadership and team development capabilities
- Deep knowledge of international cuisines and modern culinary techniques

Preferred:
- International experience in luxury hotel brands (Marriott, ITC, Taj)
- Certification from a recognized culinary institution
