Job Title: Commis Chef / Junior Cook
Company: The Grand Spice Hotel
Location: Goa, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are looking for an enthusiastic Commis Chef to join our kitchen brigade, assist in food preparation, and learn from experienced chefs in a professional hotel kitchen environment.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in preparation and cooking of dishes across assigned sections (hot, cold, bakery)
- Follow recipes and presentation standards set by the Head Chef
- Maintain cleanliness and hygiene standards in the kitchen
- Assist in mise en place and stock management
- Support during high-volume service periods
- Follow HACCP and food safety regulations

Requirements:
- Diploma or degree in Hotel Management or Culinary Arts
- 0-2 years of kitchen experience in a hotel, restaurant, or catering company
- Basic knowledge of cooking techniques, knife skills, and kitchen equipment
- Strong interest in food and culinary arts
- Physical stamina and ability to work long hours and weekends
- Ability to work as part of a team in a fast-paced environment

Preferred:
- Experience with Indian, Continental, or Asian cuisine
- Knowledge of food safety certifications (FOSTAC or similar)
