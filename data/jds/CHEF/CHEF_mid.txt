Job Title: Sous Chef / Chef de Partie
Company: Aroma Fine Dining Restaurant
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 5-8 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking a skilled Sous Chef to assist the Executive Chef in managing kitchen operations, leading a section team, and ensuring consistent food quality and presentation standards.

Responsibilities:
- Assist Executive Chef in menu development, food costing, and kitchen management
- Lead a team of junior chefs and commis during service
- Maintain quality, consistency, and presentation of all dishes leaving the kitchen
- Manage food inventory, wastage control, and stock rotation
- Ensure full compliance with food safety and hygiene regulations
- Handle kitchen operations during Executive Chef's absence
- Train and mentor junior kitchen staff

Requirements:
- Degree or diploma in Culinary Arts or Hotel Management
- 5-8 years of culinary experience with at least 2 years in a supervisory role
- Strong expertise in one or more cuisine specializations
- Proven ability to manage food cost and kitchen operations
- Leadership and team management skills
- Deep knowledge of HACCP, food safety, and kitchen hygiene standards

Preferred:
- International kitchen experience or exposure to Michelin-star environments
- Expertise in molecular gastronomy or specialty pastry
