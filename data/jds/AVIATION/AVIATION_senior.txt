Job Title: Director of Flight Operations / Aviation Head
Company: IndiaAir Express
Location: Hyderabad, India
Experience Required: 15+ years

Role Overview:
We are seeking a Director of Flight Operations to oversee all flight and ground operations, ensure regulatory compliance, and drive safety and efficiency across our growing domestic and international network.

Responsibilities:
- Lead and manage flight operations including scheduling, dispatch, and crew management
- Ensure full compliance with DGCA, ICAO, and FAA standards
- Oversee Safety Management System and drive a safety-first culture
- Manage operational control center and coordinate with MRO, engineering, and commercial teams
- Lead incident investigation and corrective action programs
- Represent the airline in DGCA audits and industry forums
- Drive digital transformation in flight operations and dispatch systems

Requirements:
- ATP License (ATPL) or senior airline management background
- 15+ years in aviation with at least 5 years in a senior operations role
- Deep expertise in flight operations, safety management, and regulatory compliance
- Proven leadership of large multi-disciplinary teams
- Strong strategic thinking and crisis management capabilities
- Excellent stakeholder and regulatory relationship management

Preferred:
- Experience at a scheduled airline operating international routes
- Exposure to IOSA certification and airline operations audits
