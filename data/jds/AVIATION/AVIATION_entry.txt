Job Title: Junior Aviation Ground Staff / Airport Operations Officer
Company: SkyLink Aviation Services
Location: Delhi, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring entry-level aviation professionals to support ground operations, passenger handling, and aircraft servicing activities at international and domestic airports.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in passenger check-in, boarding, and baggage handling operations
- Support ramp operations, aircraft turnaround, and ground equipment coordination
- Ensure compliance with DGCA and airport authority regulations
- Coordinate with flight operations, ATC, and security teams
- Maintain accurate flight logs and ground handling records
- Provide customer assistance during delays and disruptions

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Aviation Management, Hospitality, or Science
- 0-2 years of experience in airport or aviation operations
- Valid AVSEC training certification preferred
- Excellent communication skills in English and Hindi
- Ability to work in shifts including nights and weekends
- Physical fitness and ability to work in outdoor environments

Preferred:
- Diploma in Airport Management or Airline Operations
- Exposure to airline GDS systems (Amadeus, Sabre)
