Job Title: Aviation Operations Manager
Company: AirRoute Ground Handling Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 5-8 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking an experienced Aviation Operations Manager to lead ground handling operations, ensure safety compliance, and maintain service level agreements with airline clients.

Responsibilities:
- Manage end-to-end ground handling operations including ramp, baggage, and passenger services
- Ensure strict compliance with DGCA, ICAO, and airline-specific standards
- Lead and develop a team of ground operations supervisors and staff
- Coordinate with airlines, ATC, customs, and immigration authorities
- Manage shift planning, manpower deployment, and operational KPIs
- Handle disruption management and emergency response coordination
- Drive continuous improvement in turnaround time and service quality

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Aviation Management or related field
- 5-8 years of experience in airport or ground handling operations
- Strong knowledge of DGCA regulations, ICAO Annex standards, and SMS
- Proven leadership experience in aviation or transport operations
- Excellent crisis management and decision-making skills
- Shift flexibility and ability to operate in high-pressure environments

Preferred:
- IATA Ground Operations certification
- Experience with hub operations at major international airports
