Job Title: Senior Business Development Manager
Company: Vertex Solutions Ltd.
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 4-8 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking a Senior BD Manager to drive enterprise sales, build strategic partnerships, and expand our client base across key markets. The ideal candidate brings a strong network and proven revenue generation track record.

Responsibilities:
- Identify, pursue, and close high-value enterprise accounts
- Develop go-to-market strategies for new products and geographies
- Build and manage strategic partnerships with channel partners and distributors
- Lead client presentations, negotiations, and contract finalization
- Collaborate with marketing, product, and delivery teams on customer acquisition
- Achieve quarterly and annual revenue targets
- Manage and mentor a junior BD team

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business or related field
- 4-8 years of experience in B2B sales or business development
- Proven track record closing enterprise deals and exceeding targets
- Strong network in target industries (IT, BFSI, manufacturing, or retail)
- Excellent negotiation, presentation, and stakeholder management skills
- Proficiency in CRM and sales analytics tools

Preferred:
- MBA from a reputed institution
- Experience selling SaaS, IT services, or consulting solutions
