Job Title: Business Development Executive
Company: Synapse Growth Partners
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a motivated Business Development Executive to identify new business opportunities, generate leads, and support the sales team in closing deals across B2B markets.

Responsibilities:
- Research and identify potential clients through online tools, LinkedIn, and industry databases
- Conduct outbound outreach via cold calls, emails, and LinkedIn messages
- Schedule discovery calls and product demos for senior BD managers
- Maintain lead pipeline and update CRM with accurate prospect information
- Support proposal creation, presentations, and follow-ups
- Attend industry events and networking meets to generate leads

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or any discipline
- 0-2 years of experience in sales or business development
- Strong communication and persuasion skills
- Familiarity with CRM tools (HubSpot, Salesforce)
- Self-motivated with a target-driven mindset
- Ability to research and qualify leads effectively

Preferred:
- Experience in SaaS, IT services, or consulting sales
- Knowledge of LinkedIn Sales Navigator or outreach automation tools
