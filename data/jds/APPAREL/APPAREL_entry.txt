Job Title: Junior Fashion Designer / Apparel Merchandiser Trainee
Company: Stitch & Style Fashion House
Location: Delhi, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring a creative and detail-oriented junior professional to support our design and merchandising teams in developing seasonal collections and coordinating production.

Responsibilities:
- Assist senior designers in creating sketches and design concepts
- Research latest fashion trends and competitor collections
- Support fabric selection, color palette development, and trim sourcing
- Coordinate with vendors and production teams on sample development
- Maintain design files, mood boards, and sample records
- Assist in photoshoots and merchandising displays

Requirements:
- Diploma or Bachelor's degree in Fashion Design, Textile Design, or Apparel Merchandising
- 0-2 years of experience in fashion design or apparel industry
- Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and CorelDRAW
- Strong sense of color, fabric, and trend awareness
- Basic understanding of garment construction and pattern making
- Good communication and teamwork skills

Preferred:
- Internship experience with a fashion label or textile manufacturer
- Knowledge of sustainable or eco-friendly fabric sourcing
