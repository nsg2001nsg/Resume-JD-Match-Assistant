Job Title: Head of Design & Merchandising
Company: Luxe Apparel Brands India
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 10+ years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a visionary Head of Design and Merchandising to lead our creative direction, product strategy, and go-to-market planning across our portfolio of premium apparel brands.

Responsibilities:
- Define seasonal design direction aligned with brand identity and market trends
- Lead a team of designers, merchandisers, and product developers
- Drive the entire product lifecycle from concept to retail floor
- Build and manage relationships with key fabric and manufacturing partners
- Develop merchandise plans and assortment strategies across categories
- Collaborate with marketing, retail, and e-commerce teams
- Monitor sell-through data and adjust range plans accordingly

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's in Fashion Design or related field
- 10+ years in apparel design, merchandising, or product management
- Proven track record launching successful seasonal collections
- Strong leadership, creative vision, and commercial acumen
- Deep understanding of retail metrics, margin management, and consumer insights
- Excellent stakeholder management and cross-functional leadership

Preferred:
- International brand experience or exposure to luxury fashion
- Understanding of D2C apparel business models
