Job Title: Apparel Product Development Manager
Company: Trendsetter Garments Ltd.
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 4-7 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking an experienced Product Development Manager to lead end-to-end development of our apparel lines from concept to production-ready samples, ensuring quality, cost, and timeline targets are met.

Responsibilities:
- Lead seasonal product development from trend research to final samples
- Manage relationships with fabric mills, trim suppliers, and garment manufacturers
- Develop tech packs, BOMs, and production specifications
- Coordinate fittings, quality approvals, and sealing of samples
- Monitor costs and negotiate with vendors to achieve margin targets
- Collaborate with design, buying, and retail teams
- Track development calendars and ensure on-time delivery

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's in Fashion Design, Textile Technology, or Apparel Management
- 4-7 years in apparel product development or merchandising
- Strong technical knowledge of garment construction, fabric behavior, and QC
- Experience managing vendor ecosystems and production timelines
- Proficiency in PLM tools, Adobe Suite, and Excel
- Excellent negotiation and project management skills

Preferred:
- Experience with export-oriented garment manufacturing
- Knowledge of international retail compliance and testing standards
