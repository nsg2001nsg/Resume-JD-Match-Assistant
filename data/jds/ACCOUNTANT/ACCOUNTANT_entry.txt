Job Title: Junior Accountant
Company: FinServe Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a Junior Accountant to support our finance team in day-to-day accounting operations. The ideal candidate will have a solid foundation in accounting principles and be eager to grow within a dynamic financial environment.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in maintaining accurate financial records and ledgers
- Process accounts payable and receivable transactions
- Reconcile bank statements and general ledger accounts
- Prepare basic financial reports and summaries
- Support month-end and year-end closing activities
- Assist with GST filings and TDS computations
- Maintain vouchers, receipts, and documentation

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Commerce, Accounting, or Finance (B.Com preferred)
- 0-2 years of experience in accounting or finance
- Proficiency in Tally ERP or similar accounting software
- Basic knowledge of MS Excel including VLOOKUP and pivot tables
- Familiarity with GST, TDS, and Indian taxation norms
- Strong attention to detail and numerical accuracy
- Good communication and organizational skills

Preferred:
- CA Inter or pursuing ICWA is a plus
- Knowledge of SAP or QuickBooks is advantageous
