Job Title: Finance Controller / Chief Accountant
Company: Veridian Industries Ltd.
Location: Delhi NCR, India
Experience Required: 8+ years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a seasoned Finance Controller to lead our accounting function, drive financial strategy, and ensure complete statutory compliance. This is a senior leadership role with direct reporting to the CFO.

Responsibilities:
- Lead and manage the entire accounting and finance team
- Oversee financial close processes, consolidation, and group reporting
- Establish and enforce accounting policies aligned with Ind AS / IFRS
- Drive budgeting, forecasting, and long-range financial planning
- Manage relationships with external auditors, banks, and regulatory bodies
- Ensure compliance with Companies Act, SEBI regulations, and tax laws
- Provide strategic financial insights to executive leadership
- Implement ERP systems and financial automation initiatives

Requirements:
- CA (Chartered Accountant) qualification mandatory
- 8+ years of progressive experience in accounting and finance
- Deep expertise in Ind AS, IFRS, and Indian taxation
- Proven experience managing audits and regulatory filings at scale
- Strong leadership and team management capabilities
- Experience with ERP systems such as SAP S/4HANA or Oracle
- Excellent stakeholder management and executive communication skills

Preferred:
- Experience in listed company environment
- CPA or CMA certification is a plus
