Job Title: Senior Accountant
Company: Meridian Capital Group
Location: Pune, India
Experience Required: 3-6 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking an experienced Senior Accountant to manage core financial operations, ensure regulatory compliance, and support strategic financial planning. You will work closely with the Finance Manager and cross-functional teams.

Responsibilities:
- Manage full-cycle accounting including journal entries, reconciliations, and reporting
- Oversee accounts payable, accounts receivable, and payroll processing
- Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements
- Ensure compliance with GST, TDS, income tax, and ROC filings
- Coordinate with auditors during statutory and internal audits
- Analyze financial data and provide variance reports to management
- Implement and improve accounting procedures and internal controls

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Commerce or Accounting
- 3-6 years of hands-on accounting experience
- Proficiency in Tally Prime, SAP, or Oracle Financials
- Strong knowledge of Indian GAAP and taxation laws
- Advanced Excel skills including financial modeling
- Experience handling audit coordination and financial reporting
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills

Preferred:
- CA (Inter or Final) qualification preferred
- Experience in manufacturing or services sector
