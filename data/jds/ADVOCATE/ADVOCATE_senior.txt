Job Title: Senior Partner / Principal Advocate

Experience Required: 12+ years

- 12+ years of distinguished legal practice
- Established practice in corporate law, M&A, or constitutional litigation
- Proven track record before Supreme Court or High Courts

- Deep expertise in at least one specialized domain

Preferred:
