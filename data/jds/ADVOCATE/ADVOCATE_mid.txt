Job Title: Advocate / Legal Counsel
Company: Arora Legal Partners
Location: Delhi, India
Experience Required: 4-7 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking a skilled Advocate with strong litigation experience across civil, commercial, or corporate matters. The candidate will independently handle cases, mentor junior associates, and manage client relationships.

Responsibilities:
- Independently manage and argue cases before District Courts, High Courts
- Draft complex legal documents including agreements, MOUs, and pleadings
- Advise clients on legal risks and strategic options
- Conduct negotiations and represent clients in dispute resolution
- Supervise and guide junior advocates and paralegals
- Stay updated on legislative changes and landmark judgments
- Coordinate with external counsel and regulatory authorities

Requirements:
- LLB mandatory; LLM preferred
- 4-7 years of active litigation or corporate legal practice
- Strong knowledge of civil, commercial, and corporate law
- Proven experience in High Court or specialized tribunal proceedings
- Excellent drafting and negotiation skills
- Strong analytical reasoning and case strategy skills
- Ability to manage multiple matters independently

Preferred:
- Experience in arbitration and alternative dispute resolution
- Knowledge of IP law, insolvency law, or competition law
