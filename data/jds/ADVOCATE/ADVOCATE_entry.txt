Job Title: Junior Advocate / Legal Associate
Company: Mehta & Associates Law Firm
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring a Junior Advocate to support our litigation and advisory team. The candidate will assist senior advocates in case preparation, legal research, and court filings across civil and commercial matters.

Responsibilities:
- Conduct legal research and case law analysis
- Draft petitions, plaints, replies, and legal notices
- Assist in preparation of case files and court submissions
- Attend court hearings and assist senior counsel
- Maintain case records and track hearing schedules
- Support contract drafting and review tasks
- Liaise with clients for document collection and updates

Requirements:
- LLB degree from a recognized university
- 0-2 years of experience in litigation or legal advisory
- Enrolled with Bar Council of India
- Strong research skills and knowledge of Indian legal procedure
- Proficiency in drafting legal documents
- Familiarity with CPC, CrPC, Contract Act, and Evidence Act
- Good written and verbal communication in English and Hindi

Preferred:
- LLM or specialization in commercial or civil law
- Experience with case management software
