Job Title: Finance Manager / Investment Associate
Company: Venture Capital Partners India
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 4-8 years

- 4-8 years in investment banking, private equity, or corporate finance
- Advanced financial modeling and valuation expertise
- Strong knowledge of investment analysis, M&A, and capital markets
- Excellent analytical reasoning and written communication skills
- Proficiency with Bloomberg, Excel, and financial databases

Preferred:
- CFA charterholder or pursuing Level II/III
- Prior experience in PE/VC or investment banking at a top firm
