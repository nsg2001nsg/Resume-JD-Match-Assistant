Job Title: Financial Analyst Trainee / Junior Finance Analyst
Company: Spectrum Capital Advisors
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring a Junior Finance Analyst to support our investment research, financial modeling, and client reporting functions within our wealth management and corporate finance practice.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in building financial models, DCF valuations, and scenario analyses

- Prepare client reports, investment memos, and presentations

- Support senior analysts in deal due diligence and transaction support

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Commerce, or MBA (Finance)
- 0-2 years of experience in finance, investment research, or banking
- Strong financial modeling skills in MS Excel
- Basic understanding of equity markets, financial statements, and valuation
- Strong analytical thinking and attention to detail
- Excellent communication skills

Preferred:
- CFA Level I passed or pursuing
- Exposure to Bloomberg, Reuters, or Refinitiv terminals
