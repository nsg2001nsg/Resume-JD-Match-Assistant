Job Title: CFO / Head of Finance
Company: Nexgen Financial Group
Location: Delhi, India
Experience Required: 15+ years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a CFO to lead our finance function, drive financial strategy, manage investor relations, and support the organization's growth through capital raising and M&A activity.

Responsibilities:
- Lead all financial planning, reporting, treasury, and compliance functions
- Drive annual budgeting, long-range planning, and financial forecasting
- Manage investor relations, board reporting, and capital allocation
- Oversee fund raising, debt financing, and M&A transaction support
- Ensure compliance with SEBI, Companies Act, RBI, and tax regulations
- Build and lead a high-performing finance team
- Partner with CEO and board on strategic decisions

Requirements:
- CA qualification or MBA Finance from a globally recognized institution
- 15+ years of progressive finance leadership experience
- Proven experience in capital markets, M&A, or PE-backed environments
- Deep expertise in financial strategy, treasury management, and compliance
- Strong board-level communication and stakeholder management skills
- Experience leading teams through growth, restructuring, or IPO processes

Preferred:
- Prior CFO or Finance Director experience at a listed or VC-backed company
- CFA or CPA qualification is a plus
