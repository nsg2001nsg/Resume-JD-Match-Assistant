Job Title: Clinical Manager / Healthcare Operations Manager
Company: Apollo Diagnostics & Hospital Services
Location: Chennai, India
Experience Required: 5-8 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking a Clinical Manager to oversee day-to-day healthcare operations, manage clinical staff, drive quality outcomes, and ensure regulatory compliance across our hospital division.

Responsibilities:
- Manage daily clinical operations including OPD, IPD, and emergency services
- Lead a team of nurses, coordinators, and allied health professionals
- Drive patient satisfaction, safety, and quality improvement programs
- Ensure compliance with NABH accreditation standards and clinical protocols
- Coordinate with department heads on capacity planning and resource management
- Manage clinical budgets and cost optimization initiatives
- Handle escalated patient grievances and critical incident management

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Healthcare Management, Nursing, or Life Sciences
- 5-8 years of clinical or healthcare operations experience
- Deep knowledge of NABH standards and healthcare regulatory requirements
- Strong leadership and team management capabilities
- Excellent patient-centric communication and conflict resolution skills
- Data-driven approach to quality metrics and operational performance

Preferred:
- MBA in Healthcare Management
- Experience with JCI accreditation or international healthcare standards
