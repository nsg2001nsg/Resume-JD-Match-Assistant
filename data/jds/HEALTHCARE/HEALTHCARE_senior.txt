Job Title: Medical Director / Chief Clinical Officer
Company: National Health Systems Ltd.
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 15+ years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a Medical Director to provide clinical leadership across our hospital network, drive clinical quality standards, and partner with organizational leadership on healthcare strategy.

Responsibilities:
- Provide clinical leadership and governance across all hospital departments
- Define and enforce clinical protocols, patient safety standards, and quality frameworks
- Lead peer review, credentialing, and medical staff governance processes
- Drive clinical outcomes improvement and infection control programs
- Partner with the CEO and Board on organizational healthcare strategy
- Represent the hospital in regulatory, accreditation, and public health forums
- Mentor clinical leadership and build the medical talent pipeline

Requirements:
- MBBS with MD/MS specialization from a recognized institution
- 15+ years of clinical practice with at least 5 years in clinical leadership
- Proven expertise in hospital administration, quality management, and patient safety
- Deep knowledge of NABH, JCI, and Indian healthcare regulatory frameworks
- Strong leadership, governance, and executive communication capabilities
- Track record leading quality transformation in complex hospital systems

Preferred:
- Postgraduate qualification in Hospital Administration (MHA or MBA)
- Published clinical research or academic contributions
