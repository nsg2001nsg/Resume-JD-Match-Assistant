Job Title: Healthcare Associate / Junior Clinical Coordinator
Company: MedCare Hospital Group
Location: Hyderabad, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring a Healthcare Associate to support clinical and administrative operations, coordinate patient care activities, and ensure a smooth and compassionate patient experience across our facilities.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in patient registration, scheduling, and discharge coordination
- Support clinical staff in maintaining medical records and documentation
- Coordinate diagnostic tests, referrals, and follow-up appointments
- Liaise between patients, families, and clinical teams
- Maintain compliance with healthcare regulations and patient privacy standards
- Support quality improvement and patient satisfaction initiatives

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Healthcare Administration, Life Sciences, or Nursing
- 0-2 years of experience in a healthcare setting
- Basic understanding of clinical workflows and medical terminology
- Strong interpersonal skills and empathy for patients
- Proficiency in hospital information systems or EMR software
- Attention to detail and ability to work in fast-paced clinical environments

Preferred:
- Experience with EMR systems (Practo, Cerner, or Epic)
- Basic knowledge of insurance and TPA processes
