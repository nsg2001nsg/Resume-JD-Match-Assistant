Job Title: Head of Design / Design Director
Company: Innovate Labs
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 10+ years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a Design Director to lead our design organization, define the product design vision, build a world-class design team, and ensure design excellence across all our digital products.

Responsibilities:
- Define and drive the design vision, strategy, and culture for the organization
- Lead a team of product designers, UX researchers, and visual designers
- Establish design systems, standards, and quality benchmarks
- Partner with CPO and CTO on product strategy and roadmap
- Champion user-centered design across all business functions
- Build and mentor a high-performing design talent pipeline
- Represent design at executive and board level discussions

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's in Design, HCI, or related field
- 10+ years of design experience with at least 3 years leading design teams
- Outstanding portfolio demonstrating impact across complex digital products
- Deep expertise in UX strategy, design systems, and design operations
- Strong leadership, vision-setting, and executive communication skills
- Experience scaling design functions in product-first organizations

Preferred:
- Prior experience as Head of Design at a tech startup or digital product company
- Expertise in 0-to-1 product design and platform design
