Job Title: Junior UX/UI Designer / Graphic Designer
Company: PixelCraft Design Studio
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a Junior Designer to join our creative team, assist in designing digital and print assets, and contribute to building intuitive user experiences for web and mobile products.

Responsibilities:
- Create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes for web and mobile applications
- Design visual assets including banners, social media graphics, and marketing materials
- Collaborate with product managers and developers on UX requirements
- Participate in user research and usability testing activities
- Maintain design consistency using established style guides and design systems
- Iterate on designs based on feedback and testing insights

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Design, Visual Communication, or related field
- 0-2 years of experience in UX/UI or graphic design
- Proficiency in Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop, and Illustrator
- Basic understanding of UX principles, typography, and color theory
- Portfolio demonstrating design thinking and visual craft
- Good communication and collaborative skills

Preferred:
- Experience with motion graphics or micro-interactions
- Basic knowledge of HTML/CSS is a plus
