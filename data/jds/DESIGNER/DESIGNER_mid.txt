Job Title: Senior UX Designer / Product Designer
Company: Thoughtworks Digital
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 4-8 years

Role Overview:
We are seeking a Senior Product Designer to lead UX design for complex digital products, drive design strategy, and collaborate closely with product and engineering teams to ship exceptional user experiences.

Responsibilities:
- Own end-to-end UX design process from research to delivery
- Conduct user interviews, define personas, and create user journey maps
- Design and prototype interactions for web and mobile applications
- Establish and maintain design systems and component libraries
- Collaborate with product managers on feature prioritization and requirements
- Review and provide feedback on junior designers' work
- Present design decisions and rationale to senior stakeholders

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's in Design or Human-Computer Interaction
- 4-8 years of product or UX design experience
- Expert-level proficiency in Figma or Sketch
- Strong portfolio demonstrating research-led, data-informed design
- Deep knowledge of accessibility standards and responsive design
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills

Preferred:
- Experience with design for fintech, healthtech, or enterprise software
- Familiarity with design tokens, headless CMS, or design ops
