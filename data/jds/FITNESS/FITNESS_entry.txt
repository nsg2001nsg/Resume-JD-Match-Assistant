Job Title: Fitness Trainer / Personal Training Associate
Company: IronCore Fitness Studios
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a certified Fitness Trainer to conduct group and personal training sessions, help members achieve their fitness goals, and promote a motivating gym environment.

Responsibilities:
- Design and conduct personalized fitness programs for members
- Lead group fitness classes including strength, cardio, HIIT, and yoga
- Perform fitness assessments and track member progress
- Educate members on exercise techniques, nutrition basics, and injury prevention
- Maintain gym equipment cleanliness and safety standards
- Drive membership renewals and personal training package sales

Requirements:
- Diploma or certification in Fitness Training, Sports Science, or Physical Education
- 0-2 years of experience as a gym instructor or personal trainer
- Certification from recognized body (ACE, NASM, ACSM, or ISSA)
- Strong knowledge of exercise physiology, anatomy, and training principles
- Excellent communication and motivational skills
- Physically fit and passionate about health and wellness

Preferred:
- Specialization in weight management, sports performance, or rehabilitation
- CPR and first aid certification
