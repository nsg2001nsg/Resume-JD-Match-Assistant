Job Title: Banking Associate / Relationship Officer Trainee
Company: Horizon National Bank
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

We are hiring Banking Associates to support branch operations, assist customers with financial products, and contribute to meeting branch targets across deposits, loans, and investment products.

- 0-2 years of experience in banking or financial services
- Good understanding of banking products including savings, current, FD, and loans
- Strong numerical aptitude and attention to detail
- Excellent customer service and communication skills
- Ability to work in target-driven environments

Preferred:
- JAIIB/CAIIB qualification is advantageous
- Experience with core banking systems (Finacle, Flexcube)
