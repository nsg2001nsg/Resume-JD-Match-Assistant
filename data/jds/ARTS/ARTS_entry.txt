Job Title: Junior Visual Artist / Creative Associate
Company: Canvas Collective Studio
Location: Mumbai, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a talented junior artist to support our creative team in developing original artwork, illustrations, and visual content for exhibitions, publications, and brand collaborations.

Responsibilities:
- Create original artworks, illustrations, and design assets
- Assist senior artists in conceptualization and production of large-scale projects
- Prepare artwork files for print and digital formats
- Support installation and exhibition setup
- Maintain art supplies inventory and studio organization
- Research artistic references and create mood boards

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Fine Arts, Visual Arts, or Graphic Design
- 0-2 years of studio or agency experience
- Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign)
- Strong drawing, illustration, and composition skills
- Portfolio demonstrating creative range and artistic vision
- Passion for contemporary art and design trends

Preferred:
- Experience with digital painting tools (Procreate, Wacom)
- Exposure to murals, installations, or public art projects
