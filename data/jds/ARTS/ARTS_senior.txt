Job Title: Director of Arts Programs
Company: National Cultural Arts Foundation
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 12+ years

Role Overview:
We are seeking a senior arts professional to lead our national arts programming, build institutional partnerships, and establish the foundation as a leading platform for contemporary and traditional Indian arts.

Responsibilities:
- Lead the organization's arts strategy, programming, and exhibition calendar
- Build partnerships with museums, galleries, artists, and government bodies
- Manage grants, sponsorships, and arts funding programs
- Oversee a team of curators, educators, and program coordinators
- Drive artist residency, fellowship, and mentorship programs
- Represent the organization in national and international arts forums

Requirements:
- Master's or Ph.D. in Fine Arts, Art History, or Cultural Studies
- 12+ years of leadership in arts administration or cultural programming
- Deep network in the Indian and international arts ecosystem
- Proven track record in grant writing, arts fundraising, and sponsorship
- Excellent leadership, communication, and public speaking skills
- Strong understanding of both traditional and contemporary arts

Preferred:
- Experience with government cultural bodies or major arts institutions
- International exhibition or residency management experience
