Job Title: Graduate Engineer Trainee / Junior Mechanical Engineer
Company: TechFab Industries Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Pune, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring Graduate Engineer Trainees to support our manufacturing and product engineering teams in design, production planning, and quality assurance activities.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in engineering design and drawing preparation using AutoCAD or SolidWorks
- Support manufacturing process planning and work instruction development
- Participate in quality control inspections and root cause analysis
- Maintain engineering documentation and change control records
- Coordinate with production and procurement teams on engineering requirements
- Assist in testing, validation, and performance evaluation of components

Requirements:
- B.E. or B.Tech in Mechanical, Production, or Manufacturing Engineering
- 0-2 years of experience or internship in manufacturing or engineering
- Proficiency in AutoCAD, SolidWorks, or CATIA
- Basic knowledge of GD&T, machining processes, and materials science
- Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings
- Good analytical and problem-solving skills

Preferred:
- Exposure to IATF 16949 or ISO 9001 quality systems
- Knowledge of lean manufacturing or Six Sigma basics
