Job Title: Senior Mechanical Engineer / Design Engineer
Company: Precision Engineering Works
Location: Chennai, India
Experience Required: 5-9 years

Role Overview:
We are looking for a Senior Engineer to lead mechanical design and product development projects, mentor junior engineers, and drive continuous improvement in our manufacturing operations.

Responsibilities:
- Lead mechanical design and development of new products from concept to production
- Conduct design reviews, FEA analysis, and design for manufacturability assessments
- Manage engineering change orders and design documentation
- Collaborate with manufacturing, quality, and procurement teams
- Identify and implement cost reduction and process improvement opportunities
- Mentor junior engineers and provide technical guidance

Requirements:
- B.E. or M.Tech in Mechanical or Manufacturing Engineering
- 5-9 years of engineering experience in design or manufacturing
- Expert proficiency in SolidWorks, CATIA, or NX CAD tools
- Strong knowledge of GD&T, FEA, and manufacturing processes
- Experience with DFMEA, PFMEA, and PPAP documentation
- Excellent analytical, communication, and project management skills

Preferred:
- Exposure to automotive or aerospace manufacturing
- Six Sigma Green Belt or Black Belt certification
