Job Title: Regional Sales Manager / Key Account Manager
Company: Apex Commercial Products Ltd.
Location: Hyderabad, India
Experience Required: 5-8 years

We are looking for a Regional Sales Manager to drive revenue across our assigned territory, manage key accounts, lead a field sales team, and expand our market share in the region.

Responsibilities:

- Manage and grow a portfolio of key enterprise and institutional accounts

- Collaborate with marketing, product, and supply chain teams
- Prepare and present regional sales forecasts and performance reports

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business or related field
- 5-8 years of B2B sales experience with at least 2 years in team management
- Strong track record achieving and exceeding revenue targets
- Excellent key account management and customer relationship skills
- Channel sales and distribution management experience
- Proficiency in CRM tools and sales analytics platforms

Preferred:
- MBA from a recognized institution
- Experience in FMCG, industrial, or technology sales
