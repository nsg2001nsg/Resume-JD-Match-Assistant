Job Title: Sales Executive / Inside Sales Representative
Company: TechSell Solutions
Location: Bangalore, India
Experience Required: 0-2 years

Role Overview:
We are hiring Sales Executives to drive new customer acquisition, manage the full sales cycle from lead to close, and contribute to revenue targets across our B2B product portfolio.

Responsibilities:
- Identify and qualify prospects through cold calls, emails, and LinkedIn outreach
- Conduct product demonstrations and solution presentations
- Follow up on inbound leads and manage pipeline in CRM
- Achieve monthly sales targets and conversion KPIs
- Maintain accurate records of sales activities and customer interactions
- Collaborate with marketing for campaign follow-ups and lead nurturing

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in any discipline
- 0-2 years of sales experience (inside or field sales)
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills
- Strong persuasion, negotiation, and objection-handling skills
- Self-motivated and target-driven approach
- Familiarity with CRM tools (Salesforce, Zoho CRM, or HubSpot)

Preferred:
- Experience in software, SaaS, or technology sales
- Knowledge of sales frameworks (SPIN, MEDDIC, or Challenger Sale)
